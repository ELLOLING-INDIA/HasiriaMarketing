Our History

A Journey of
20+ Years

Hasiria Marketing Sdn Bhd was established in 2003 in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. From humble beginnings as a local spice trader, we have grown into one of Malaysia's most trusted importers and exporters of premium food products and general merchandise.

Our remarkable 20+ year journey is built on an unwavering commitment to quality, honest partnerships, and a deep understanding of the global spice and food trade. Over the years, we have cultivated active export operations in Australia and Singapore, and are now strategically expanding into the vast China market.

With exceptional leadership and a clear vision, we have maintained steady growth — achieving significant milestones in sales and market penetration while helping develop Malaysia's industrial and export base in the new millennium.
