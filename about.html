Our Journey

20+ Years of
Global Agro Commerce

Established in 2008, Hasiria Marketing Sdn Bhd began as a focused domestic trading operation and has grown into a multi-market import and export powerhouse. We specialise in sourcing, processing and distributing high-quality spices and agro-based food products across Malaysia and globally.

Our global network has enabled us to establish active export channels to Australia and Singapore, with an ambitious strategic push now underway into China — driven by our UK-based director's international business development expertise.

Driven by quality, integrity and customer satisfaction, we strive to be a trusted partner in the global agro and food products industry.
