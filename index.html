Founded in 2003, Hasiria Marketing Sdn Bhd has grown into one of Malaysia's most respected importers and exporters of premium spices, pulses, food products, and general merchandise.

With over two decades of expertise and strong partnerships across Australia, Singapore, and now expanding into China, we bring the finest Malaysian produce to global tables.
