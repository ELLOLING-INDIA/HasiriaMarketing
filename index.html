Who We Are

A Dynamic & Growing
Agro Trading Company

Established in 2008, Hasiria Marketing Sdn Bhd is a dynamic and growing company specialising in the sourcing, processing and distribution of high-quality spices and agro-based food products. With a strong foundation in the agro industry, we are committed to delivering excellence across both domestic and international markets.

Our core expertise lies in the import and export of a wide range of agro products, supported by a reliable global network. In addition to bulk trading, we provide professional repacking services for spices, tailored to meet the needs of retail businesses — ensuring product freshness, hygiene and appealing presentation.
